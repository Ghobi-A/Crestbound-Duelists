# Story Outline

Four-act structure. Compact by design. See LORE_BIBLE.md for world
rules and characters.

## Act I — The Crest at Greymere

Locations: Greymere, the Hollow Court, Veyrhold.

Peaceful introduction to Greymere → the restricted Crest site is
disturbed → Aren, trusting someone they shouldn't have, ends up inside
the Hollow Court → trained candidates and hostile intruders fail to
bind the dormant Crest; it accepts Aren → Avelainian authorities
intervene; removal would kill Aren → the non-choice: permanent state
custody or the sanctioned **Duelist Trial** → Aren enters the Trial at
Veyrhold → **Serin Dain** introduced.

## Act II — The Quiet Arms Race

Locations: Bellgrave, Caer Lumen, Solharbor.

Kingdoms quietly competing for Crest power; sanctioned vs Unbound
Duelists; forbidden research; first evidence of **Riven Crests**;
rising political tension; Aren and Serin become reluctant allies.

## Act III — Fracture

Open political escalation. A betrayal inside the group; a meaningful
death or major tragedy — the consequence of Aren's misplaced trust.
Major break between Aren and Serin. **Cassian Orre** revealed as the
hand accelerating the crisis, and his plan exposed:

1. Increase tension → 2. force kingdoms to mobilise hidden Crest
resources → 3. locate the world's important Crests → 4. gather or
expose them → 5. destroy them all.

## Act IV — The First Warden

The truth about Eidros and **the Veil**: the Crests are also the
containment system. Former enemies ally; confrontation with Cassian;
final Crest crisis. Cassian is defeated — he may sacrifice himself to
stop the consequences of his own plan, without erasing his
responsibility. His final understanding: *"I still believe humanity
will misuse this power. I was wrong to believe that gave me the right
to choose the answer for everyone."*

**Ending:** optimistic but not consequence-free. The truth spreads;
monopolies crack; Crests stay contested. Aren helps found an
independent organisation to keep any single kingdom, institution, or
individual from controlling all Crest knowledge (sequel hook).

## Protagonist arc

Begins: compassion without judgement (trusts too easily, hates
treating people as adversaries). The Act III tragedy is the price.
The lesson is **not** "trusting people is stupid" — it is:

> Compassion without judgement is naivety. Judgement without
> compassion is cruelty.

Ends: discerning, decisive, resistant to manipulation — still
fundamentally compassionate. Not Serin.

## Rival arc (Serin Dain)

strangers → competitors → reluctant allies → close but argumentative
companions → ideological enemies → reconciliation.
Serin is often right; Aren is right where suspicion would have caused
needless harm. Serin defeats Aren sometimes.

## Prototype scope (current build)

Compressed opening only: Greymere → speak with Warden Elara Thorne
(and optionally Mira Solen) → the Hollow Court arch → short dialogue →
3v3 tactical battle against a Riven-touched Raider, Hexbound Adept,
and Unbound Mercenary → post-battle scene: the dormant Crest has
responded to Aren; something larger has begun.

Deliberately **not** in the prototype: the Duelist Trial, Veyrhold,
the other kingdoms, Serin's introduction, Cassian, Riven Crest lore
beyond one enemy's name, the Veil.

## Player collection (design direction)

Crests (builds), Crest fragments (awakening/lore), Bonded Entities
(earned through bonds, trials, sites, and story — never a capture
mechanic), and a small number of recruitable Duelists. First game
scope: 3–6 major party members, not hundreds.

## Death rules

Major characters may die in the story; no gameplay permadeath for the
core party in the first game. A dead Duelist's Crest usually goes
dormant; its Entity usually vanishes (rare Echoes exist — later).
