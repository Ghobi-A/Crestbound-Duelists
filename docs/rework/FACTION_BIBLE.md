# Crestbound — Faction Bible Draft

## Duchy of Avelan

**Philosophy:** stability through restraint.

A conservative, administrative state that presents regulation as protection. Avelan fears uncontrolled Crest power, but its restraint is compromised by secret archives, sanctioned Duelists and the desire to preserve authority.

## Draco Factum

**Philosophy:** security through strength.

A militarised state whose current name may have been adopted after a revolution, conquest or ideological transformation. Its exact constitutional form remains open. It treats Crest power as a strategic resource and argues that restraint leaves nations vulnerable.

## Republic of Prudentia

**Philosophy:** security through knowledge and competent governance.

A scholarly, technocratic republic that believes dangerous knowledge should be controlled by people qualified to understand its consequences. Its public ideal is responsibility; its danger is allowing an institution to define competence for everyone else.

## Crown of Opulena

**Philosophy:** stability through commerce and interdependence.

A wealthy maritime power whose exact constitutional form remains open. It believes trade prevents war more reliably than moral agreements. Opulena applies the same logic to Crests: demand will exist, so regulation and profit are preferable to prohibition.

## Cassian's position

Cassian Clave sees four different systems producing the same result: every power claims a respectable reason to retain control. His answer is to remove the foundation rather than transfer ownership. The tragedy is that he treats a correct diagnosis as permission to impose an absolute solution.
