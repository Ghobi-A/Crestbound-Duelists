# Crestbound — Story Master Draft

> Working draft. This document is not canon until the story pass is complete.

## Premise

Kai lives in Greymere Town in the Duchy of Avelan, close to the sealed ruins of the Hollow Court. When the seal is disturbed, Kai becomes bonded to a Crest that rejects trained candidates and accepts Kai instead. The bond cannot be removed without killing Kai, forcing the Avelan authorities to decide whether Kai is a citizen to protect, a weapon to control, or a danger to contain.

Kai is taken to Civara, where the sanctioned Duelist system can assess and contain the bond. From there, Kai is drawn into a conflict between four political powers, each claiming to know how dangerous Crest power should be governed. The deeper truth is that the Crests are fragments of Eidros, the First Warden, and part of an ancient containment system whose failure could destabilise the entire world.

## Central question

Who should be trusted with power that no institution fully understands: the state, experts, markets, individuals, or nobody at all?

## Protagonist tension

Kai begins with a small-community moral framework. In Greymere Town, he can judge people through direct knowledge of their circumstances. The wider world exposes the limits of that method. Institutions can cause harm through policy, incentives and distributed responsibility even when many individuals inside them are sincere. Kai's maturation is learning to understand systems without abandoning individual judgement.

Cassian Clave represents the opposite failure. He sees systems so clearly that individual distinctions begin to disappear. Kai starts too zoomed-in; Cassian ends too zoomed-out.

## Current principal cast

- **Kai** — newly Crestbound protagonist.
- **Rin** — rival, competitor and eventual reluctant ally.
- **Warden Almyra** — Avelan authority overseeing Kai and the opening incident.
- **Liora Sen** — Crest scholar investigating the historical record.
- **Cassian Clave** — antagonist who concludes that every existing system will misuse Crest power.
- **Eidros, the First Warden** — dead origin of the Crests and the hidden architecture behind them.

## Opening movement

Greymere Town presents ordinary life before the story widens into politics. Kai's connection to Joey gives the opening personal stakes, while Lena, Elder Silas, Gell and Danfor establish how ordinary people understand the Court and the rules surrounding Crests.

The Hollow Court incident breaks that local world. Kai is accepted by the dormant Crest after others fail to bind it. Almyra must bring Kai under official protection, while Liora recognises that the event contradicts accepted Crest history. The first battle reveals that the Court was not disturbed accidentally, and the bond gives Avelan a reason to move Kai to Civara before rival powers learn what happened.

## Long arc

The story currently follows four broad movements: the Crest incident in Avelan, the Duelist system and political competition, the fracture caused by betrayal and Riven Crest research, and the revelation that the Crests are also part of the Veil. These movements remain provisional and will be rewritten after the character and faction passes.

## Opening story pass — provisional

### Kai before the Crest

Kai's life in Greymere Town should establish a real attachment to the place before the plot removes him from it. Joey is the most direct personal connection: someone who knows Kai without treating him as a future Duelist, chosen one or political asset. Lena, Elder Silas, Gell and Danfor make the town feel lived in and show different attitudes toward the Hollow Court: practical fear, inherited memory, curiosity and official caution.

Kai should not begin with a grand ambition to become a Duelist. The opening works better if the Crest is an interruption that Kai would not have chosen. The early conflict is therefore about losing an ordinary future rather than gaining a heroic destiny.

### The Hollow Court incident

The seal is disturbed by people who know enough about the Court to enter it, but not enough to understand what they are waking. Almyra arrives because Avelan has been monitoring the site or because an earlier warning reached Danfor. Liora is present for a scholarly reason that puts her at odds with the official account.

Inside the Court, trained candidates and intruders attempt to reach the dormant Crest. The Crest rejects them. Kai enters for a personal reason connected to Joey, the disturbance or someone in immediate danger, and is accepted without understanding why. The bond produces a visible response that makes removal impossible.

### Why Kai is taken to Civara

Kai is not taken to Civara simply because the city is larger. Civara contains the Avelan institutions capable of examining a new Crest bond, deciding whether Kai is legally a Duelist and preventing an international incident. Almyra frames the transfer as protection. Kai experiences it as removal from home and separation from Joey.

The transfer creates the first central tension: Avelan may be saving Kai from exploitation while also claiming ownership over Kai's body and future. Liora wants access to the historical anomaly. Almyra wants control of the crisis. Kai wants to return to Greymere. Rin can enter the story in Civara as someone who believes Kai has been given an opportunity, a danger or an unfair advantage.

## Greymere opening sequence — scene spine

This is a provisional scene order, intended to protect the relationships before the plot accelerates.

1. **A day Kai already likes.** Kai handles an ordinary job in town. Joey interrupts with a bad idea; Kai refuses, then gets involved. Their ease and Kai's private mischief establish that he is not waiting to be rescued from Greymere.
2. **The small rules of home.** Danfor catches them somewhere inconvenient. At Gell's shop, Gell delivers a confident but imperfect account of Civara and the foreign powers while Joey steals something minor. Kai sees it and chooses not to intervene. Lena later treats an everyday injury and scolds Joey without ceremony.
3. **Silas at the ridge.** Elder Silas asks whether Kai has visited the old ridge. He refuses to explain the question. A subtle abnormality follows: animals react to the hill, water tastes metallic or a minor tremor passes through town. Kai notices that Silas is afraid.
4. **The official inspection.** Almyra arrives before the disaster with legitimate paperwork for a geological or seal inspection. Danfor accepts the procedure. Almyra and Silas recognise each other. Kai notices the exchange but has no context for it. Movement around the Court becomes restricted, creating an immediate friction between Danfor's duty, Lena's practical concerns and Almyra's authority.
5. **The Court opens.** A disturbance inside the Hollow Court becomes an emergency rather than a lore lecture. People are hurt; the threat is concrete. Kai goes inside because someone he knows is in danger or because Joey is implicated, not because he wants adventure.
6. **The unwanted bond.** Trained candidates or responders fail to claim the dormant Crest. Kai is accepted. The bond is painful, visible and irreversible. His first use of the power protects someone but also causes damage, making the event morally complicated from the start.
7. **The town survives.** The Court is contained, but Greymere is damaged rather than erased. Danfor keeps order, Lena treats the wounded, Joey reacts emotionally and Silas understands that an old history has become present again. Almyra gives Kai an incomplete account of what happened.
8. **The transfer bargain.** Avelan orders Kai to Civara for examination, legal classification and containment. Kai resists being treated as property. Almyra offers a constrained autonomy bargain—training, equipment and protection in exchange for cooperation, with a meaningful right to leave—while withholding facts she considers unsafe to disclose.
9. **Departure.** Kai leaves Greymere with Joey's unresolved dependency and Lena's practical instructions rather than a grand farewell speech. The town remains a place he can return to, which makes the separation more painful and the later return more useful.
10. **Civara's first judgment.** Rin sees an untrained civilian carrying an extraordinary Crest and concludes that Kai got lucky. Liora sees a historical anomaly. Kai encounters a world where the people around him may be sincere while the systems they serve still constrain or exploit them.

The opening should leave three mysteries active without resolving them through exposition: why Almyra knew Silas, why Avelan was watching Greymere, and why the Crest accepted Kai. None of these should imply that Kai's entire life was secretly scripted.
