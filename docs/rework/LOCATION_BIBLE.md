# Crestbound — Location Bible Draft

## Greymere Town

A quiet settlement in the Duchy of Avelan. It establishes Kai's ordinary life, relationships and local assumptions before the Crest incident breaks them.

## The Hollow Court

A sealed ruin beneath or near Greymere. It is publicly treated as an empty historical site, but contains dormant Crest power, forbidden records and evidence that the opening incident was planned or anticipated.

## Civara

An Avelan city associated with the sanctioned Duelist system. It is where Kai's private crisis becomes an official political and institutional problem.

## Belgrave

A Draco Factum frontier city where Crest militarisation is visible rather than hidden.

## Veritas

A Prudentian archive city whose knowledge institutions control much of the surviving Crest record.

## Solharbor

An Opulenan maritime city built around trade, diplomacy, smuggling and regulated or illegal Crest markets.

## Evershade

An older settlement outside the main political blocs that preserves Crest traditions predating the modern states.

## The First Vault

A late-story site connected to Eidros, the Veil and the original fragmentation of the Crests.
