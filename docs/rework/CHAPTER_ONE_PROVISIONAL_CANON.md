# Chapter One — Provisional Canon

Status: **provisional canon** (2026-11), written for the vertical slice
(`docs/VERTICAL_SLICE_SPEC.md`). It fills the gaps the protected
documents leave open for the opening chapter. It does **not** change
`docs/LORE_BIBLE.md`, `docs/STORY_OUTLINE.md` or
`docs/GAME_DESIGN_FOUNDATION.md`. Where this note and a protected
document disagree, the protected document wins.

It uses the current naming set: Kai, Warden Almyra, Liora Sen, Joey,
Lena, Danfor, Elder Silas, Gell, Avelan, Civara.

## What stays unrevealed

- What the Hollow Court Crest is, and why it accepted Kai.
- Why Almyra and Silas know each other.
- Why Avelan was watching Greymere.
- Eidros's death, the original catastrophe, and whatever lies beyond
  the Veil.
- Reserved for later chapters: the Riven Entity's "Still?", Cassian
  Clave, Rin and the first duel with him, and anything connecting Kai's
  family to the Court.

Nothing in Chapter One implies that Kai's life was scripted or that he is
a chosen one. He goes into the Court for Joey.

## The chapter follows STORY_OUTLINE.md

1. **Greymere baseline.** Kai's ordinary life, with Joey, Lena, Danfor,
   Silas and Gell.
2. **Quiet abnormal signs**, rationalised by everyone except Silas.
3. **Almyra arrives** with a small Avelan specialist unit, under cover of
   a geological inspection.
4. **A structural failure** opens the Court. The specialists enter, a
   bonding attempt fails, the passages seal, and Riven manifestations
   appear.
5. **Joey is trapped below.** Kai goes in to bring him out.
6. **The involuntary bond and the impossible memory.**
7. **Protective custody or Duelist classification.** Kai chooses
   classification because it keeps the most autonomy, and only after
   securing the right to leave.
8. **Civara classification:** the Crest answers all six patterns, and
   the player chooses which one Kai stabilises.

## New material

### Why Joey was below

For years Joey has slipped through a crack in the ridge hollow to take
small bronze "found things" (fittings, studs, a cracked bell-tongue) and
sell them to Gell, who never asks where they came from. When Avelan's
cordon goes up, Joey assumes his stash will be confiscated and goes back
for it at dusk. The structural failure traps him below.

This fits his canon pattern: Joey creates a problem, Kai removes the
consequence, and Joey promises to change.

### Specialist Corwen Hale

- **Who he is:** a containment specialist in Almyra's unit.
- **Build:** Sorcerer, Eclipse Crest, Grave Stag.
- **Voice:** dry, procedural and quietly brave. He narrates his own
  mistakes in report language ("Bonding attempt: unsuccessful. Cause:
  me.").
- **His part in the incident:** he is the specialist whose sanctioned
  bonding attempt fails and wakes the Court's systems. He feels
  responsible, and he fights beside Kai and Almyra at the climax.
- **Mysteries:** he knows nothing about Silas or about Kai's records.

### Old Greymaw

- **What it is:** an ancient grey wolf from Greymere Outskirts folklore.
  Children are told it is "older than the road". It is ordinary fauna:
  not Riven, not an Entity, not Crest-bearing.
- **Why it's dangerous now:** since the resonance beneath the ridge
  stirred, it has been frenzied and bold. It comes down to the old ridge
  path at dusk, and its eyes catch a Crest-gold light.
- **In the story:** it foreshadows "animals react to the hill" without
  new supernatural lore.
- **How players learn of it:** Old Ferris's rumour, tracks, torn hedges
  and a scattered camp.
- **After the chapter:** whether it is fought or not has no story
  consequence beyond local talk. Defeating it gives Kai a fang that
  Ferris says "remembers the ridge".

### The Hollow Court's interior

The Court is "older, drowned, radial and subtly oriented toward its
dormant Crest node" (ART_BIBLE). Its three spaces:

- **The Collapsed Stair:** the new breach. Avelan survey pegs, a dropped
  lantern, the specialists' rope line.
- **The Drowned Gallery:** still black water and statues of robed figures
  in a ring. Earlier, failed bonding attempts left scorch-marked plinths.
  Almyra is pinned beneath fallen masonry.
- **The Radial Chamber:** six worn monuments around the dormant node.
  It visually echoes the chamber in *The Last Accord*, but is never
  stated to be the same place.

### Riven manifestations in the Court

The Court's ancient systems produce them when the bonding fails. They are
resonance given shape, not creatures with a will.

| Manifestation | Description |
|---|---|
| **Riven Wisp** | a flickering knot of distorted light |
| **Riven Husk** | resonance poured into a fallen statue |
| **Riven Anchor** | a pillar of sealed light holding the passages shut; breaking it reopens the way |
| **The Sealed Choir** | the climax: the Court's containment rite turned hostile, voices in the stone with a gold core |

Each takes on the voices and shape of the place. None speaks to Kai or
recognises him.

### The bond and the impossible memory

Kai reaches Joey and the dormant Crest as the chamber collapses. The
Crest bonds to him without conventional conscious acceptance. He hears
something calling and, remembering Silas, does not answer, and it takes
him anyway.

**The memory:**
- The Radial Chamber before its ruin, whole, lamplit and full of robed
  figures holding a single note.
- Someone at the centre lowering a hand onto the node, saying a word Kai
  cannot hear.
- Then the note breaks.

**What it never shows:**
- faces;
- Eidros's death;
- the catastrophe;
- the Veil.

**After the memory:**
- His first use of the power shields Joey, and cracks the chamber.
- Almyra's specialists find the Crest integrated too deeply to remove.
- Silas recognises something about it and refuses to explain.

### The custody conversation

In Greymere, after the climax, Almyra sets out the two legal paths:
protective custody, or Duelist classification.

- The player can press her: on custody, on records, on why Avelan was
  here.
- She answers partially and withholds what she considers unsafe.
- Kai chooses classification, and only after securing the right to leave
  the arrangement.
- The conversation has no branching ending. The choices shape tone and
  what Kai learns, not the outcome.

### Civara

- **What the player sees:** only the Classification Hall and its
  forecourt.
- **Who is there:** an Avelan clerk and the assessors.
- **Liora Sen** attends as Prudentian expertise and first meets Kai here.
  She is fascinated by the result, not yet by him.
- **What happens:** the six patterns answer, the test is repeated, and
  the player chooses.
- **The assessment:** a short combat assessment against an Avelan
  assessor of the same class demonstrates the stabilised pattern.
- **Reserved:** Rin and the Trial belong to later chapters.

### Supporting details

- **Danfor's spar:** Danfor runs a weekly sparring lesson "because if
  you two are going to be stupid, you'll be stupid with your guard up".
  Danfor has no Crest. Sanctioned Duelists are rare in Greymere.
- **Joey's age and voice:** an adult, Kai's lifelong friend.
  - The old child-like line "Ma says I'm too young to go near the
    Court" is retired.
  - He still lives with his mother.
